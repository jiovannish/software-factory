# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and considerate. Critique ideas and code, not people. Welcome
questions and different perspectives, and respect privacy and consent.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, sharing private information
without permission, and sustained disruptive behavior are not welcome.

Report concerns privately to [saul@saugar.dev](mailto:saul@saugar.dev). Maintainers
will handle reports discreetly and may remove content, warn contributors, or
restrict participation according to the severity and repetition of the behavior.
These standards apply to everyone, including maintainers, across project spaces.
